# Making a wooden door hanger

I had some spill wood left after creating a re-tractable shelf with my partner. I had an idea to make something of various parts that connects in japanese style woodworking. You know those where the joints connect seamlessly without screws or bolts. 

However, I had no chisels.

So, I decided to use a Mora-knife to create one of those hangers that you can stabilize on the door directly.

## Discussion

This project took about 3 days in total.

- 1st day I cut out all three pieces and the details
- 2nd day I spent on papering. I oiled the tree at the end of the day
- 3rd day I glued the parts together.

It was a fun little project. I have no idea how much this hanger can hold without breaking, but I would guess it is about 5kg at most. 

If I would do it again, I would buy a chisel set to get more accurate cuts.
